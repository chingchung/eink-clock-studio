# Still / product brief

Focus: explore expressive monochrome clock faces and combine them with small widgets. Keep the editor minimal: face collection, live round preview, upper/lower widget selection, and a text field only when a note is selected.

Collection 001: Everyday numerals, oversized Grotesk cardinal numerals, Orbit dots, Petal geometry, Roman numerals, Radial tick marks, Equation arithmetic, Tumble tilted serifs, Cutout stencil numerals, and Now reminders. The last four take direction from user-supplied clock references. Date, digital local time, Monday-first week rhythm, and personal note are reusable widgets. Mechanical analog hands remain a separate top layer.

No accounts, integrations, device apply, calendar management, countdown, uploads, or freehand drawing. Browser-local settings only. Existing v1 artwork is not deleted or migrated. Original designs take direction from the visible typography and geometry of https://www.pinterest.com/chingchunghung/clock/; the board could only be partially inspected due to its login overlay.

Success: switch faces instantly, independently choose or remove both widgets, edit a short note, and return to the same composition after reload. Usable on desktop and mobile with native keyboard controls.

Hardware dimensions, bezel clearance, panel resolution and refresh behavior remain unverified. The browser is a visual prototype.
