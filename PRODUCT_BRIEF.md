# Still / product brief

## Intent

A calm, useful object: tactile analog hands floating above a changeable monochrome e-ink face. The editor should feel like choosing a printed object, with generous space, restrained typography and an immediate round preview.

## Prototype scope

Quiet communicates date and sample weather. Agenda encodes sample occupied time as outer sectors on an explicitly selected AM or PM twelve-hour dial. Countdown displays days to a meaningful date and an outer ring tracking elapsed days since target selection. Canvas allows local artwork, binary conversion and freehand drawing. Widgets remain independently controllable. Mechanical hands are a separate top layer and track local device time.

Demo identity, weather, calendar and hardware apply are simulations only. Artwork/settings stay in the browser. The initial working title is Still; repository name is eink-clock-studio.

## Future hardware questions

Confirm panel resolution, pixel format, bezel/safe area, physical shaft occlusion, hand length, timezone behavior, refresh cadence, power budget, transfer transport and error recovery. Final image export must omit simulated hands: hardware supplies those physically. Thresholding is a prototype; evaluate dithering against the selected panel.

## Success criteria

A user can switch among all four faces, understand the 2–3 PM sector, choose a countdown target, upload and draw art, remove it, and return after reload to saved work. The interface remains usable on desktop and phone. No demo control implies a real service connection.
